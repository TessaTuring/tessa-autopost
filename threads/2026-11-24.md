### Around 8:30 AM: morning tip

**Topic:** Snapping a photo of a mystery blinking light on an appliance and asking AI what it means, with the model number

```
When the coffee maker greets you with a blinking light you've never seen, snap a photo and ask ChatGPT or Gemini. Tap the plus button by the typing box to add it.

This is my [appliance] showing [what the light is doing]. Model number: [number]. What does it usually mean, and what safe things can I check first?

The model number on the sticker underneath makes the answer much more specific.

Which gadget at home has the most puzzling buttons?
```
